- [B-12] **Amendment to algorithms.md 5.5 (needs approval).** The 5 attempts of the specification fail for 1 708 of 1 728 combinations on the real food database (dish days and blind rotation). Implemented and documented in `algorithms.md` 5.5: the 5 attempts stay (stage 1, results unchanged, tested), then stage 2 with at most 12 more attempts: no dish, best of 8 rotation offsets per meal, carry-over of the earlier meals' errors. Tolerances are NOT relaxed. Result on the seeded data: omnivore, vegetarian and vegan plans without allergies are found for 216 of 216 combinations (4 profiles x 6 goals x 3 meal counts); omnivores are served for every single allergen tested; failures remain only for very restrictive requests (for example a 120 kg profile with more than 200 g protein on a vegan diet without gluten or soy) and give 422 `insufficient_foods`. Typical time 0.2 s, worst case about 2 s. Alternative if you prefer not to change the algorithm: relax the tolerances (constants) or reduce the food set. (status: open)
- [B-12] The diet plan goal comes from the profile (the request has no `goal`); `diet_type` defaults to `omnivore` and is not inferred from the profile; `dietary_restrictions` is stored as a free-text note and does not filter foods. Plan name `"<Goal> diet[ (<type>)], <n> meals per day"`. `meal_order` counts the meals of a day from 1, `scheduled_time` stays null until the schedule task (B-14). Items inside a meal are returned main item first, fat last (the table has no position column). (status: open)

Accepting a plan follows the training plan rules (409 if not a draft, 404 for other users, 56 days). A failed generation keeps the previous draft. (status: open)
